From Knowledge Expert to AI Hero: A Hands-On Workshop on Transforming Raw Documents into AI-Ready Knowledge

What does it take to turn business knowledge into something AI can reliably use? In this practical, hands-on workshop, we'll demonstrate how subject matter experts can shape better AI outcomes by improving the knowledge behind them.

We'll show how raw documents that are unfit for AI can be transformed into an AI-ready format. You'll see how AI automation can amplify SME expertise in quality-assuring knowledge, and how that knowledge can be enriched with the business context AI needs but source documents often leave out.

Throughout the workshop, we'll make the SME's role concrete: where their judgment matters, how automation supports their work, and how they can contribute to the knowledge foundation for enterprise AI.

You'll leave with a practical understanding of what AI-ready knowledge looks like, how experts can help create and maintain it, and how to succeed as a knowledge expert in the age of AI.
